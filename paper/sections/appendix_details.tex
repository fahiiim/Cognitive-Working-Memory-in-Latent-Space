\section{Implementation details}
\label{app:details}

\paragraph{Training.} AdamW, learning rate $10^{-3}$, weight decay $10^{-4}$, 300 warm-up steps then
cosine decay, gradient clipping at 1.0, fp32 with TF32 matrix multiplies (faster than bf16 autocast
for this launch-bound model on our GPU). T1/T2: width 128, $r=1$, batch 512, 8000 iterations,
$T_{\text{train}}=20$. T3: width 64, $r=4$, batch 32 (with random dihedral augmentation), 5000
iterations, $T_{\text{train}}=32$. Training batches are sampled on the GPU and filtered against the
ID-val and ID-test hashes. Seeds 0--4 for the base models and 0--2 for the jointly trained methods
(T1/T2); seeds 0--1 for mazes.

\paragraph{Gate hyperparameters.} Every jointly trained gate gets the same budget of three settings
on addition (seed 0), and the setting with the fewest mean ID-val steps among those within 0.5 points
of the best ID-val accuracy is used everywhere (\texttt{configs/tune\_gates.yaml}). Settings: ACT
ponder weight $\tau\in\{10^{-3},10^{-2},10^{-1}\}$; PonderNet $(\beta,\lambda_p)\in\{(0.01,0.1),(0.01,0.2),(0.1,0.05)\}$;
v1 gate compute weight $\lambda\in\{10^{-3},10^{-2},10^{-1}\}$.

\paragraph{Post-hoc heads.} Fitted on the frozen backbone for 1500 iterations (batch 256; mazes:
batch 32) on fresh training-distribution batches rolled out to $2T_{\text{train}}$ steps (backbone in
bf16 autocast, heads in fp32); Adam, learning rate $2\cdot 10^{-3}$ with cosine decay. The pooled correctness head (TRM-style) predicts exact match from the
masked mean of the latent state; the completion heads predict \cref{eq:done} per position.

\paragraph{Threshold grids.} Probability rules: $\{0.5,0.6,0.7,0.8,0.9,0.95,0.98,0.99,0.995,0.999,0.9999\}$;
entropy, KL and hidden-state change: 19 log-spaced values in $[10^{-6},1]$; stability $k\in\{2,3,5\}$;
ACT cumulative halting $\{0.5,0.7,0.9,0.95,0.99\}$; PonderNet cumulative halting
$\{0.3,0.5,0.7,0.9,0.95,0.99\}$; fixed $T\in\{1,\dots,T_{\max}\}$. Test-time budget
$T_{\max} = \lceil \max d^*/r\rceil + 16$ per split.

\paragraph{Metrics.} Premature-stop rate: share of instances halted before $\mathrm{LB}(x)$;
premature-error rate: share halted before $\mathrm{LB}(x)$ \emph{and} wrong; overthinking: steps beyond
the first step from which the answer stays right, on correctly answered instances; regret: accuracy
lost and extra steps spent relative to the stable oracle (steps on instances the oracle gets right);
depth tracking: Spearman $\rho(\text{steps}, d^*)$; length leakage: partial Spearman
$\rho(\text{steps}, L \mid d^*)$; calibration: 15-bin ECE of per-position done probabilities.
