\section{Backbone and completion heads}
\label{sec:method}

\paragraph{Backbone.} GLWM-2 is an instance of \cref{eq:local}: a pointwise embedding $E$, a
pointwise initial state $H = \mathrm{LN}$, and the shared step
\begin{equation}
  h^{(t+1)} = \mathrm{LN}\Big(h^{(t)} + W_{\mathrm{out}}\,
  \mathrm{GELU}\big(\mathrm{Conv}^{(r)}_{3}\cdots \mathrm{GELU}(\mathrm{Conv}^{(1)}_{3}[h^{(t)}; e])\big)\Big),
  \label{eq:step}
\end{equation}
with $r$ kernel-3 convolutions per step, input re-injection (``recall''), a residual connection, and a
LayerNorm over channels at every position. The decoder is a pointwise two-layer MLP. The growth rate
is therefore exactly $r$ positions per step (checked by the flip test). We use width 128 and $r=1$.
The backbone is trained with a per-step cross-entropy
averaged uniformly over $T_{\text{train}}=20$ steps (``fixed prior'') or with the progressive loss of
\citet{bansal2022endtoend}; the pilot (\cref{sec:experiments}) selects the objective per task.

\paragraph{Completion heads.} A completion head reads each position's latent state and predicts
whether that position is \emph{done}: correct now and at every later step. The target is computed from
the model's own trajectory,
\begin{equation}
  z_i^{(t)} = \prod_{s=t}^{T} \mathbb{1}\big[\hat y_i^{(s)} = y_i\big],
  \label{eq:done}
\end{equation}
i.e.\ a reversed cumulative minimum of per-position correctness, and the head is trained with binary
cross-entropy. We study three variants. \emph{Post hoc}: a pointwise MLP on $h_i^{(t)}$, fitted after the
backbone is frozen (rollouts to $2T_{\text{train}}$). \emph{Joint}: the same head trained alongside the
backbone with an auxiliary binary cross-entropy (weight 1). Unlike ACT and PonderNet, the head never
reweights the per-step task loss: the backbone still receives the same task gradient at every step,
so the head is a separate prediction problem rather than a halting distribution over the task loss. \emph{Propagating}: the head also reads its
neighbours' previous done probabilities through a kernel-3 convolution,
$p_i^{(t)} = \sigma\big(g(h_i^{(t)}) + \mathrm{Conv}_3[p^{(t-1)}, \mathbf 1]_i\big)$, which mirrors the
pending-bit automaton of \cref{prop:automaton}: a position can learn to wait until its left neighbour
reports done.

\paragraph{Halting rule.} An instance halts at the first step where every valid position is done with
probability above $\tau$ (\cref{alg:halt}); $\tau$ is selected on ID-val as the cheapest threshold whose
accuracy is within 0.5 points of the best. Because the rule takes a minimum over positions, its cost
grows with the slowest position, not with the input length.

\begin{algorithm}[t]
  \caption{Halting with local completion heads (batched; halted instances leave the batch).}
  \label{alg:halt}
  \begin{algorithmic}[1]
    \Require input $x$, backbone $(E,H,U,D)$, completion head $c$, threshold $\tau$, budget $T_{\max}$
    \State $e \gets E(x)$;\quad $h \gets H(e)$;\quad $p \gets \mathbf 0$
    \For{$t = 1,\dots,T_{\max}$}
      \State $h \gets U(h, e)$;\quad $\hat y \gets \arg\max D(h)$
      \State $p \gets \sigma\big(c(h, p)\big)$ \Comment{per position; the pointwise head ignores $p$}
      \If{$\min_{i\ \text{valid}} p_i > \tau$ \textbf{or} $t = T_{\max}$}
        \State \Return $(\hat y, t)$
      \EndIf
    \EndFor
  \end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\paragraph{Baselines.} All baselines use the same backbone and selection protocol. \emph{References}:
fixed $T$ (selected on ID-val), the oracle lower bound $\mathrm{LB}(x)$, the any-step oracle (first
step with the right answer), and the stable oracle (first step from which the answer stays right).
\emph{Post hoc}: max-softmax and entropy (minimum confidence over positions), $k$-step prediction
stability \citep{zhou2020pabee}, KL between consecutive steps and hidden-state change (maxima over
positions), maximum confidence over steps \citep{fan2025looped}, and a pooled correctness head in the
style of TRM \citep{jolicoeur2025trm} fitted post hoc. \emph{Jointly trained}: ACT
\citep{graves2016act}, PonderNet with its KL to a geometric prior \citep{banino2021pondernet}, a joint
pooled correctness head, and the v1 GLWM pooled gate with its initialisation fixed.
